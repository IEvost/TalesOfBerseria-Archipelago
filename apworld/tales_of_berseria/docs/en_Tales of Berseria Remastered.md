# Tales of Berseria Remastered

## What does randomization do to this game?

The contents of treasure chests are shuffled into the multiworld. Opening a Katz box, winning a story
battle and (optionally) picking up each sparkle point for the first time also send checks.

With **Region Keys** enabled, every region after the prologue starts locked. Map exits into a locked
region refuse to work until you receive that region's key, so the story only moves forward as keys arrive.

## What is the goal?

Defeat the final boss.

## What does another world's item look like in this game?

Opening a chest that holds another player's item shows a message such as
"Found Alice's Hookshot!" instead of the usual item window.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

A message box appears ("Received X from Player") and the item goes straight into your inventory.
